Frequently Asked Questions

How do I securely share documents? expand_more

Navigate to the 'Documents' tab, select the file, and use the 'Share via Secure Link' option to generate an encrypted access portal.

What is the typical response time? expand_more

Standard inquiries are addressed within 24 business hours. Urgent tickets marked appropriately receive attention within 4 hours.

Can I update a resolved ticket? expand_more

Resolved tickets cannot be edited. Please create a new ticket and reference the old ticket number if the issue persists.
